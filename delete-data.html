Request deletion of your data — EveryUnit

Last updated: 12 August 2026

EveryUnit has no user accounts, so there is very little to delete. This page explains exactly what exists, what you can remove yourself immediately, and how to ask us to remove the rest.

What you can delete yourself, right now

Your favourite converters, any custom converters you built, and your calculator history are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us and we have no copy of them.

To erase all of it:

  • Uninstall EveryUnit, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → EveryUnit → Storage → Clear storage

You can also clear just your calculator history from inside the app: open the calculator, tap the history icon, then Clear history.

What we may hold, and how to have it deleted

The only data that ever reaches us is a feedback message you chose to type and send using the feedback button in the app. It is stored with the date it was sent. Nothing is attached to it — no name, no email address, no device identifier.

To request deletion, email:

ahmedshafin240@gmail.com

Use the subject line “EveryUnit data deletion request”.

Because feedback messages carry no identifying information, please include enough detail for us to find yours — for example the approximate date you sent it, and a phrase from the message. Without that we may not be able to tell which message is yours.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your email, normally within a few days.
  2. We locate the matching feedback message, if one exists.
  3. We delete it and confirm to you in writing.

We aim to complete requests within 30 days. If we cannot find a matching message, we will tell you that rather than leaving the request open.

Advertising data

EveryUnit shows ads through Google AdMob. Any advertising data associated with your device is held by Google, not by us, so we cannot delete it on your behalf. You control it directly on your device:

  • Settings → Google → Ads — here you can delete or reset your Advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • Google's own controls are at myadcenter.google.com.
