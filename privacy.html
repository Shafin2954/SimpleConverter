Privacy Policy for EveryUnit

Last updated: 12 August 2026

EveryUnit is a unit converter and calculator for Android. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information. It is written to describe this specific app, not a generic template.

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no profile.
  • Your conversions, favourites, custom converters and calculator history stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects advertising identifiers. That is the main way data leaves your device.
  • The only other outbound requests are currency exchange rates, and feedback you choose to type and send.

What stays on your device

The following are saved locally using Android's standard app storage and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

DataWhy
Favourite convertersSo your chosen converters appear first.
Custom converters you createSo your formulas persist between sessions.
Calculator history (last 20 calculations)So you can reuse a previous calculation.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

EveryUnit displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and coarse location inferred from IP address;
  • ad interaction events, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped.

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us — we do not receive or store it. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and you can review Google's advertising practices at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device, which also lets you opt out of ad personalisation.

Currency exchange rates

When you open the currency converter, the app requests current exchange rates from exchangerate.host. This request contains only currency codes and an API key belonging to the app. It contains no personal information and no identifier for you or your device beyond the network-level IP address inherent to any internet request.

Feedback you choose to send

The app includes an optional feedback form. If — and only if — you type a message and tap send, that message and a timestamp are transmitted to a private Google Apps Script endpoint operated by the developer, so that feature requests can be read and acted on.

Please do not include personal information in feedback messages. Whatever you type is what gets sent; the app does not attach your name, email address, or any device identifier to it.

Children

EveryUnit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app serves advertising, it is rated for a general audience.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no account data, so there is nothing to delete on our side except feedback messages you have voluntarily sent. To have a feedback message removed, email the address below and describe it. Everything else the app stores lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
